# Roadmap

Two phases. Dates are indicative.

## Phase 1 · 2026–2027: MVP development and validation

Image-level data from the five founding resources, Core Schema v0, a public proof-of-concept search API and portal, and governance and sustainability planning. See [Phase 1](phase-1.md).

## Phase 2 · 2027–2028: Adoption and scaling

What we expect to take on once the proof of concept has been tested:

- Automated harvesting of image-level metadata
- Onboarding and certification for repositories beyond the founding five
- Additional funders and a sustainable operating model
